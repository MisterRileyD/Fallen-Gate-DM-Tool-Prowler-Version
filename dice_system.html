Main Stats

Dice System
MAIN STATS Body: Body represents your overall health, strength, and constitution. This is your "Hit Points" stat, and is usually reduced by taking damage and by casting Blood Spells. Mind: Mind represents your intelligence, how much mana you have, and your ability to learn certain spells. These are your "Spell-Points" which are usually expended to cast most spells and is reduced by attacks which target your intelligence and alertness. Spirit: Your soul represents the ability to empathize, your healing strength, along with your courage. This you your measure of inner strength, it is used to boost healing spells (expend 2 Spirit to restore 1 extra Body) and by attacks which cause fear or self-doubt.

Combat Modifier

Dice System
COMBAT MODIFIER Combat Modifier: Bonus Points based on Rank that are added to your Combat Roll due to your prowess and skill in battle with spells, weapons or even unarmed combat. 1. Points are added to the Attacker's Combat Roll when performing a physical or magical attack. 2. Points are added to the Defender's Combat Roll when defending against a physical or magical attack. COMBAT MODIFIER DOES NOT MODIFY DC ROLLS Combat Modifier is determined by your Rank found in ⁠ranks-and-stats on Discord Combat Modifier 'In-Use' Example: Attacker Rank is Dark Knight with Combat Modifier of +2 Defender Rank is a Praetorian with Combat Modifier of +4 1. Dark Knight performs a Combat Roll to attack and makes a 7 2. Praetorian performs a Combat Roll to defend and makes a 6 3. Dark Knight has modifier of +1; 7 + 2 = 9 4. Praetorian has modifier of +3; 6 + 4 = 10 5. Dark Knight’s attack misses and Praetorian emotes successfully defending against the attack.

Low Stat Effects and Death

Dice System
LOW STAT EFFECTS If a stat is reduced to zero in combat, it can have certain effects: Body: If your Body is reduced to zero in combat, you are too injured to continue and thus out of the battle unless you are healed. Mind: If your Mind is reduced to zero in combat, you become light-headed or so mentally drained that you collapse from mental exhaustion and thus out of the battle unless you are refreshed. Spirit: If your Spirit is drained, you will lose all hope or faith that the battle can be won and retreat. The battle is declared over, and you have lost. NOTE: You cannot lower a stat to 0 via spell use. Example: Calling upon Blood Magic until your body stat is 0 is not allowed. The most you could lower that stat to is 1. DEATH Once you are at 0 on one or more stats, you are incapacitated. Any further attack will render you as Dead. At that point, only resurrection-type spells can bring you back, i.e., Anastasis (Resurrection), Corpus Reformationis (The Body of Reformation), Ortus Phoenix (Rise of the Phoenix).

Combat Dice

Dice System
COMBAT DICE TERMS Armor Class (AC): Strength of natural/donned armor worn. The Attacker's Combat Roll must meet or exceed the Defender's AC during a physical attack. If the Attacker doesn’t beat the Defender's AC, the attack fails. If the Attacker meets or exceeds the AC, the Defender will Combat Roll to dodge the attack. Initiative Roll: Determines who takes action first in an encounter using /roll100 (d100). Combat Roll: Determines the outcome of an attack using /roll20 (d20). Difficulty Class (DC) Roll: In certain circumstances, the Defender is required to make a DC Roll once an attack is successful to avoid further collateral damage using /roll20 (d20). Combat starts by everyone making an Initiative Roll. The person with the higher roll starts the round with an action. Initiative Roll Example: 1. Demon A and Demon B engage in combat. 2. Both Demon A and Demon B make an Initiative Roll. 3. Demon A uses /roll and makes a 62. 4. Demon B uses /roll and makes a 48. 5. Demon A attacks first. General Combat Example: 1. Demon A casts a spell at Demon B. 2. Demon A uses /roll20 to attack and makes a 3. 3. Demon B uses /roll20 to defend and makes a 5. 4. Demon B avoids the attack. The Order and ‘Emoting Rhythm’ of a Combat Encounter: 1. The Attacker emotes an attack, leaving the outcome open ended. 2. The Attacker posts the spell description; specifically damage the spell will cause and any other info the DM needs, i.e., Auto Hit, DC Roll requirements, etc. 3. The Attacker makes a Combat Roll. 4. The Defender makes a Combat Roll. 5. Bonuses/Buffs are applied and a winner is determined. 6. If the Attacker wins and the spell or action also has a DC associated with it, the Defender then uses a /roll20 to beat the DC. 7. The 'Victor' of the roll emotes the results. 8. Both the Attacker and Defender post their revised stats as a result of the encounter.

Critical Fails and Automatic Hits

Dice System
CRITICAL FAILS AND AUTOMATIC HITS ON COMBAT ROLL YOU ARE THE ATTACKER Automatic Hit: Attacker rolls a twenty (20) on a Combat Roll. 1. Attack hits regardless of Combat Modifiers, Defender's AC, or defense roll. 2. Damage from the ability is doubled. 3. If the attack has a DC condition, the Defender automatically fails the DC Roll as well. YOU ARE THE DEFENDER Counter Strike: Defender rolls a twenty (20) on a Combat Roll. 1. Defender can ‘counter strike’ the Attacker. 2. Counter strikes must be similar to the attack initiated; if the Attacker performs a physical attack, then the Defender must counter likewise. If the Attacker casts a spell, the Defender can choose to deflect their spell back at the Attacker. 3. Defender will act as the Attacker during the counter strike and follow the normal order of the combat encounter. Example 1; Physical/Weapons 1. Attacker throws a spear at the Defender rolls a 7. 2. Defender rolls a 20. 3. Attacker misses and the Defender may counter strike IF they are able to counter with a similar physical attack. In this instance, the Attacker struck from a distance, the Defender must posses a weapon to counter from a distance. 4. Initial Attacker defends as normal against the counter attack. Example 2; Spells 1. Attacker casts 'Lightning' and rolls a 10. 2. Defender rolls a 20. 3. Spell is deflected back to the Attacker and will Auto Hit the Attacker as listed in the Spell Description, Defender buffs are not added to the attack roll. 4. Attacker will DC Roll to avoid any lasting effects from the spell (when applicable). NOTE: The DM of the RP has final say on all determinations/rulings regarding counter strikes!

Combat Specifics

Dice System
COMBAT SPECIFICS TAKING DAMAGE If struck with an attack you take damage to any of the one or more of your three stats, as prescribed in each character's Special Abilities or Weapon Description (physical attacks) or Spell Description (magical attacks). Psychic attacks would target your Mind . . . a bite or sword attack or a fireball would damage your Body . . . a fear spell however would attack your Spirit. RULING ON TIE ROLLS 1. In the event of a tie roll between two people of equal rank, the Attacker wins. 2. In the event there is a tie roll between two people not of equal rank, the person with the higher Rank wins. NOTE: ‘Higher Rank’ means the person with the higher stats listed in ⁠ranks-and-stats on Discord. This principle also applies to Players vs. Monsters. ARMOR AC AND HOW IT WORKS When you are NOT wearing armor, any Attacker’s Combat Roll from a physical attack that beats your Combat Roll to defend will land. Example when not wearing armor: 1. Attacker uses /roll20 and makes a 4. 2. Defender uses /roll20 and make a 3 to avoid the attack. 3. Attacker’s roll wins, therefore their physical attack hits the Defender. When wearing armor, the total combined number of the Attacker's Combat Roll plus all Combat Modifiers must be equal or higher than the AC score it order for the attack to penetrate the armor. Example when wearing armor: 1. Defender is wearing armor giving them an AC of 7 (AC: 7). 1. Attacker uses /roll20 and makes a 4. 2. Attacker’s roll did not overcome the Defender’s AC: 7, therefore the attack fails UNLESS the Attacker’s Combat Modifiers make the final total of their roll equal or higher than the Defender’s AC: 7. REMINDER: Armor does NOT protect you from magic, only physical attacks.

1s, 20s, and AC

Dice System
1s, 20s and AC How do rolling Nat 1s, Nat 20s, and AC interact with each other? Failing to hit the AC ends the attack. No roll to defend is made. Rolling a Nat 20 to hit ends the action and resolves the attack at double damage, etc. no roll to defend is made. Rolling a Nat 1 to attack ends the action. No roll to defend is made. You can only counter when you roll a Nat 20 on a roll to defend. Under the above circumstances, no roll to defend is made. No counter attack is possible.

Curses, Judgements, Magic, and Potions

Dice System
RULES CONCERNING CURSES/JUDGEMENTS MAGIC AND POTIONS CURSES/JUDGEMENTS Curse Magic and Judgement Magic follow the same rules. Due to the powerful nature of these types of spells, only one person a day can be affected by a Curse or Judgment spell. ’Once-A-Day’ use of Curse/Judgement Example: 1. Archangel A casts "Occidere Judicii" Judgement against Demon B. 2. Judgement spell is an Auto Hit with a DC attribute of 11 (DC: 7) 3. Demon B makes a DC using /roll20 and gets an 8, thus beating a DC: 7, therefore Demon B avoided the Judgement. 4. Archangel A (or any other Family member) cannot cast any more Judgements for 24 hours. 5. One (1) Curse is still available to cast, but Archangel A cannot be the one to cast it. NOTE: One (1) Curse and one (1) Judgement TOTAL in a 24 hour period; this means that if anyone in the Family casts a Curse, then no other Curses can be cast by anyone in the Family for 24 hours. Also, the person who cast the Curse cannot cast a Judgement for the 24 hour period either. POTIONS Potions are quite powerful and because of this along with their magical effects, cannot be used one after another. Therefore, one (1) potion per 24 hours is the rule. Potions can be used to restore Mind and/or Body and/or Spirit from a score of zero (0) IF the potion is crafted to do so. Undead characters and creatures, such as Vampire and Lich player characters, can consume all potions except Refresh and Healing potions.

Dice System

Praetorian: 30 Minute Recovery Time for all stats. Keeper of Knowledge: 5 Minute Recovery Time for all stats. High Priest/ess: 5 Minute Recovery Time for all stats. Princess of Darkness: 5 Minute Recovery Time for all stats. The Triumvirate: Instantly recovery out of battle. For example: 1. Praetorian 1 and Praetorian 2 have finished combat. 2. Praetorian 1 is at 19/20/20 3. Praetorian 2 is at 3/7/20 4. 30 minutes later, both Praetorian are back at 20/20/20 USING MAGIC OUT OF COMBAT When using magical abilities out of battle, you still must account for your use of Body / Mind / Spirit points. Your recovery time is based on the rank chart listed above.
