Steps

  1. Enter a name for your event.
  2. Click the "Add" button to add a new player, monster, or dragon to the encounter.
  3. Select the type of monster, dragon, or the player's rank.
  4. Adjust the stat values (Body, Mind, Spirit, Bonus) and add any AC.
  5. Select if the player is eligible for the loot split. (Our general rule is that the DM does not earn loot from any encounters that they manage.)
  6. After the Init Rolls have been made, enter those values for all the players and monsters. This should populate the Map and the Battle Summary section with all of the current stats displayed and be in the correct turn order.
  7. As the event unfolds, update the stats as player/monsters take damage and use spells. The updated stat values should be displayed in the Battle Summary section. If a stat value falls to zero, that player/monster will be removed from the Battle Summary section.
  8. As the event unfolds, click and drag the characters on the map to show the current positions.
  9. Use the Battle Summary and Map sections to keep track of the encounter.
  10. If you need to share the encounter, copy the Battle Summary manually or take a screenshot of the map.
  11. After the event is over, click the "Show/Divide Loot" button to generate the loot drops for all the monsters that have a body stat equal to zero. The loot will also be split among the players eligible for loot. (randomly)
